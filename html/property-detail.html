About This Property

Welcome to this stunning architectural masterpiece in the heart of Koramangala, Bangalore. This modern glass villa offers unparalleled luxury and breathtaking views from every angle. Seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living, this property is designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

The open-concept main floor features soaring 14-foot ceilings, floor-to-ceiling retractable glass walls, and a state-of-the-art chef's kitchen imported from Italy. The primary suite is a true sanctuary, occupying its own wing with a private terrace, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-like bathroom enveloped in Calacatta marble.

Step outside to discover a private oasis complete with a zero-edge infinity pool, outdoor kitchen, fire pit, and manicured terraced gardens perfectly scaled for intimate gatherings or lavish entertaining.
